ProfileSeg v3.1 — review packet

Version 3.1.0, branch profileseg-v3. Consolidated after six review rounds; the full record (withdrawn claims, superseded analyses, responses per round) is in docs/PROFILESEG_V3_REVIEW_HISTORY.md. Every table here is generated from result files under benchmarks/results/ (generation script: benchmarks/profileseg_review_tables.py for the calibration blocks; the others were copied programmatically from the JSON files named in each section). "Seg" means region-level screening of differential profiles, not segmentation of curves into intervals.

1. Summary

ProfileSeg compares signal profiles around predefined anchors (TSS, summits, nucleosome dyads) between two conditions with biological replicates, and reports amplitude, shape and a translation-type effect separately. The questions it addresses: did the spatial distribution of signal at a region change, and, where a translation describes the change, by how many bp. The statistical core is a per-region permutation null pooled across regions, needed because 2 or 3 replicates per group give only 3 or 10 label assignments per region.

2. Method

  • Inputs. Sample manifest (condition, replicate, optional batch) with bigWig or BAM signal files (BAM: fragment midpoints, coverage or read ends; MAPQ, duplicate, proper-pair and fragment-size filters), or a precomputed matrix; a BED of predefined anchors with strand; window, bin and background settings.
  • Shape tests on background-corrected, mass-normalised profiles: shape_kernel (MMD-type kernel distance between condition means, scaled by within-condition spread plus a label-invariant s0); shift (projection of each sample on the pooled template's derivative; directional); shape_any (per-region Cauchy combination of the two p-values, then one BH — approximately valid through a tail approximation, calibration checked by simulation).
  • Null. Statistics of all non-observed label assignments, pooled over regions; in v3 pooled within quantile strata of each region's total spread (identical under every assignment) when each stratum keeps ≥ 200 regions and ≥ 2000 null values. The output reports the number of testable regions, strata and null values.
  • Translation description. estimate = cross-correlation shift of the condition means (all lags). A direction (Higher/Lower) and bp value are given only when it agrees in sign with the projection, explains ≥ 50 % of the between-condition difference (translation_r2) and is unambiguous (translation_ambiguity < 0.9); otherwise Changed, NaN bp and the reason in translation_flag. translation_fit (high_r2 ≥ 0.8 / moderate_r2) is descriptive. The earlier per-sample template fit is kept as shift_bp_fit.
  • Diagnostics. Depth per region (exact for BAM, estimated for bigWig), replicate influence, batch confounding (tests refused when condition and batch coincide), fragment-length QC for BAM.

7. Limitations and open items

  1. Independent real data (not used during development), with real-count comparison to diffSplice.
  2. The yeast MNase excess: unresolved; replicate-structure diagnostic proposed for the output.
  3. Distinguishing translations from other redistributions (R² does not; a model comparison is a candidate).
  4. Selection-adjusted or interval estimates for the bp effect.
  5. Per-class calibration with few regions (a null that adjusts for the covariate without coarsening the p-value grid).
  6. Per-region absolute amplitude test.
  7. Spatial dependence beyond duplicated windows; multimodal and asymmetric real shapes beyond the semi-real cells.
  8. All simulation evidence comes from one simulator family developed alongside the method.
