Scope of the claims

  • These are empirical results in the simulated and semi-real scenarios listed below. "ok" means that no statistically clear exceedance of the nominal level was observed in a cell (the 95 % Monte Carlo CI of the mean FDP did not lie above 0.05). It does not establish FDR ≤ 0.05.
  • The covariate-stratified null (§4) does not depend on the labels, so the exchangeability of label assignments within a region is untouched. Comparability of the null across regions is a separate, empirical property; it is improved, not guaranteed.

1. Protocol

Fixed before the first run on seeds 5 000 000+: parametric shapes, 600 regions, 20 % changed (half translated by 100 bp, half broadened ×1.5), 2 vs 2 and 3 vs 3, 100 runs per cell. One factor varied at a time: peak width (sd 60 / 150 / 400 bp), depth (5 / 15 / 40 / 160 reads per bin at the apex), replicate noise (low / default / high), share of changed regions (0 / 5 / 20 / 50 %), overlapping windows (every region duplicated 200 bp away), mixed region classes (narrow, broad, low depth; 200 each, reported per class), a balanced sample-wide 15 bp artefact, real replicate noise (GSE112560). Outcomes at nominal FDR 0.05: mean FDP with 95 % Monte Carlo CI (FWER with an exact binomial CI under the complete null); power; p01 = share of unchanged regions with p < 0.01 (nominal 0.01).

Deviations from the original protocol: runs per cell raised from 30 to 100 after a one-run smoke test; mixed_large (1000 regions per class) added after the first run; the v3 null was designed after the first run. Development runs therefore reuse the seeds the design was developed on. The confirmatory run (§6) uses new seeds and criteria fixed before it was run.

4. Per-class tail calibration and the v3 null

With the v2.4 null (one null pooled over all regions), p-values of unchanged regions were not comparable across region types: narrow peaks ≈ 2× liberal in shape_kernel, broad peaks fully conservative, low-depth regions ≈ 1.6× liberal in shift. The whole-list FDR stayed near nominal because conservative and liberal classes offset each other. Cause: F = D / (W + s0) with one global s0 is compressed where a region's spread is small relative to s0 and heavy-tailed where it is large.

v3 pools the null within quantile strata of each region's total spread (identical under every label assignment), only when each stratum keeps ≥ 200 regions and ≥ 2000 null values (a coarser p-value grid otherwise prevents BH from calling small sets). Rejected alternatives: location–scale standardisation along the covariate; a 2-D grid with a noise-whiteness covariate.

5. Per-class FDR and stratified BH

Global BH targets the FDR of the whole list (formal control requires valid input p-values and suitable dependence; here calibration is assessed empirically). A class with almost no power (broad peaks, power ≤ 0.06) collects mostly false calls even with calibrated p-values. Stratified BH targets the FDR within each stratum, but (i) only strata that match the subgroup help, and (ii) the combined list is then not FDR-controlled at the same level: two strata with one true null each give 1 − 0.95² ≈ 0.0975 at level 0.05 (the implementation reproduces 0.099 in 20 000 draws).

7. What can and cannot be claimed

  • Can: in the scenarios tested (width, depth, noise, share changed, overlapping windows, balanced artefacts, real noise; 2 and 3 replicates), whole-list FDR estimates are close to or below 0.05 with no clear exceedance, reproduced on new seeds; per-class tails within the pre-specified tolerance at 3000 regions (and from 1000 regions at 3 vs 3; at 2 vs 2 only from ~2000 testable regions, see the round-3 sweep in benchmarks/results/round3/), i.e. within ~1.5× of nominal for all three shape tests.
  • Cannot: a proven FDR bound; exact permutation validity across regions; FDR within a subgroup (predefined or post hoc) under global BH; per-class calibration with a few hundred regions; anything about multimodal or asymmetric real shapes beyond the semi-real cell; per-region absolute amplitude, which is miscalibrated under complete nulls in two designs (FWER = FDR 0.20 and 0.275).
  • Real data: PRO-seq 3 vs 3 is within nominal, but on yeast MNase 2 vs 2 (all 18 within-genotype splits) shape_kernel has FDP 0.11–0.13 and calls in 17 % of real-null runs (benchmarks/results/insertion_v3/). A batch explanation for part of this was proposed after seeing the results and is not established.
